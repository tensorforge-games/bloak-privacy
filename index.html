This Privacy Policy explains what information Bloak ("the game") uses, and how. By playing the game you agree to this policy.

1Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress, scores, coins, purchases, unlocked items and settings (such as sound and music) on your device. This information stays on your device. We do not create accounts and we do not collect your name, email address or phone number through the game.

2Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device type and operating system, and how you interact with ads. This may involve cookies or similar technologies.

Players in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom are asked for consent before personalized ads are used, and can change that choice at any time using the Privacy button in the game's Settings.

You can also reset or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).

More information:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • AdMob and Google Ads data practices

3In-app purchases

The game offers optional purchases, such as removing ads. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details.

4Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help.

5Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers described above (Google AdMob and Google Play), who process it under their own privacy policies.

6Data retention and deletion

Game data is stored on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data. Because we do not hold your data on our servers, there is nothing else for us to delete. For data held by Google, see Google's privacy tools.

7Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, and to object to or withdraw consent for certain processing. Because the game does not keep personal data about you on our servers, these requests mainly apply to data held by Google. You can contact us with any question.

8Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the date above updated.
